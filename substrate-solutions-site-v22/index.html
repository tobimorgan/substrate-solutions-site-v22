01 / ABOUT

THE SUBSTRATE APPROACH

Buildings are only as strong as the protection around them.

Water intrusion rarely begins with a dramatic failure. It begins at a joint, a crack, a parapet, a penetration or a poorly treated interface.

Substrate Solutions approaches waterproofing and building protection from the substrate outward—combining assessment, preparation, specification and installation to protect structures against moisture and environmental exposure.

A specialist approach to building protection.

Substrate Solutions is positioned as a specialist building-protection company focused on waterproofing, roof protection, concrete rehabilitation and building-envelope solutions.
